Why Skincare Matters in NYC

🚇 Subway & Urban Transit Pollution

  • NYC subway & traffic generate PM2.5 that settles on skin
  • Pollution clogs pores and accelerates aging
  • Can trigger sensitivity reactions & inflammation
  • Solution: Daily double cleanse + antioxidant serums

💨 Air Quality Challenges

  • Vehicles, construction & industry raise daily AQI
  • Environmental stress increases oxidative damage
  • Pollution breaks down skin barrier over time
  • Solution: Antioxidant protection (Vitamin C, green tea)

🏙️ Concrete Jungle Effect

  • Buildings & concrete reflect UV rays, amplifying exposure
  • High UV even on cloudy NYC days year-round
  • Constant reflection accelerates photoaging & sun damage
  • Solution: Daily SPF 30+ (minimum) every single day

❄️ Extreme Weather Swings

  • Harsh dry winters strip skin barrier & hydration
  • Humid summers increase sweat production & breakouts
  • Extreme seasonal shifts stress sensitive skin
  • Solution: Rotate between rich creams (winter) & lightweight gels (summer)

💧 Hard Water & Tap Quality

  • NYC tap water has high mineral & chlorine content
  • Hard water irritates sensitive skin & strips moisture
  • Mineral buildup prevents product absorption
  • Solution: Gentle cleansers + hydrating toners to rebalance skin
